# CurbDay San Diego 🚛🌴

> Curbside collection & street sweeping companion for the City of San Diego.  
> *"Wheels in, surf's up"*

---

## Features

- **Weekly Collection Schedule**: Real-time pickup schedules for Trash (Black Cart), Organics (Green Cart), and Recycling (Blue Cart).
- **ESD Holiday Slide Engine**: Automatic calculations for the City of San Diego's 5 observed ESD holidays (1-day slide).
- **Street Sweeping Reminders**: "Move Your Car" schedules by block, side, and posted enforcement hours.
- **Bin Sorter & Hazardous Waste**: Instant guidance on proper sorting and one-day pop-up HHW collection events.
- **Calendar & Email Alerts**: Subscribe to weekly reminders and iCal feed sync.
